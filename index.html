Social Postiz

A private publishing tool operated by the Social Postiz team.

Social Postiz is an internal scheduling tool used by the Social Postiz team to plan and publish short-form videos and posts to social media accounts that we own or manage on behalf of our clients, with their explicit consent.

What it does

  • Connects a social account only after the account owner logs in and authorizes it.
  • Uploads videos and captions prepared by our team and publishes them at the scheduled time.
  • Shows basic post statistics to the account owner.

What it does not do

  • It is not open to the public and does not allow sign-ups.
  • It does not sell, share or use account data for advertising.
  • It never posts content the account owner has not approved.
