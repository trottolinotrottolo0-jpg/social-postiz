Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how Social Postiz ("we"), operated by the Social Postiz team, handles information when an account owner connects a TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account to our publishing tool.

Information we access

  • Basic profile information provided by the platform (account ID, username, display name, avatar).
  • Access tokens issued by the platform, used only to publish content and read post statistics for that account.
  • The videos, images and captions that we upload on the account owner's behalf.
  • Post performance data (views, likes, comments, shares) made available by the platform's official APIs.

How we use it

Only to schedule and publish content to the connected account and to report its performance to the account owner. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share it with third parties, except service providers that host our infrastructure (for example cloud storage for media files) and only as needed to run the service.

Storage and security

Data is stored on infrastructure controlled by the Social Postiz team. Access tokens are kept private and are never shown publicly. Media files are kept only as long as needed to publish and archive the content.

Retention and deletion

An account owner can disconnect their account at any time from the platform's settings or by contacting us. When an account is disconnected we delete its access tokens; on request we delete all related data within 30 days.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and you may withdraw your consent at any time, by contacting us through the account that is connected to the service. Users in the EU have the rights provided by the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Children

The service is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
